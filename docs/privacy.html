Nifty board privacy

Nifty board does not ask you to sign in, and it does not have an account for you.

The app shows a signed range for the next Nifty cash close, plus the latest Nifty and GIFT prints. To do that, it reads the published forecast from this site and asks Yahoo and NSE IX for the public quotes. Those services see the usual connection details, such as an IP address, under their own policies.

The app does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or location. It does not show ads, and it does not sell data.
